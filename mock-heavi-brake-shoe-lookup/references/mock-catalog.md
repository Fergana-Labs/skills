## Manufacturer confirmation

- `HV-DEMO-1504-QX`: QX brake family, 15 × 4 in.
- `HV-DEMO-1657-STD`: Q-Plus brake family, 16.5 × 7 in, standard-service lining.
  Manufacturer bulletin `DEMO-SB-14` marks `HV-DEMO-1657-A` as superseded.
- `HV-DEMO-1657-EXT`: Q-Plus brake family, 16.5 × 7 in, extended-service lining.

The two 16.5 × 7 in records intentionally show why dimensions alone are insufficient.
